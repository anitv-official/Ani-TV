ANITV · PRIVACY & SECURITY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This policy explains how AniTV handles information when you use the Android application. We aim to collect and retain only what is needed to provide the features you choose to use.

Authentication and account data

AniTV supports email and password authentication and Google sign-in through the configured authentication provider. When you create or use an account, account information such as your name and email address is processed to authenticate you and maintain your session.

Favorites, history and downloads

Favorites, viewing history, preferences and download-related information are used to provide the corresponding in-app features. The application also stores selected preferences and local profile information on your device. Downloaded files remain on your device unless you remove them.

Content sources and third parties

AniTV retrieves anime and manga content from the sources exposed by the application. Those sources may receive normal network request information required to return content. Authentication is provided through Appwrite and Google when you choose those sign-in options. AniTV does not place authentication secrets or private API keys in the application.

Storage and security

Local data is stored using the Android application storage mechanisms and is protected by the platform’s normal application isolation. Network services may process data according to their own policies. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to avoid exposing sensitive credentials.

Deletion and requests

You can sign out from the application and remove locally stored data by uninstalling the application. If you need an account or server-side data deletion request, contact the project maintainers through the official repository.

Changes

We may update this policy when the application or its services change. The effective date above will be updated when a new version is published.
